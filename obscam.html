Step 3: Add to OBS

  1. In OBS, add a new Browser Source
  2. Check the "Local file" option and paste the stream link above
  3. Set width to 1280 and height to 720 (or your preferred resolution)
  4. Click OK and your phone camera should appear in OBS

Note: Keep this page open on your phone to continue streaming

This service uses WebRTC to stream your camera feed. No video data is stored on any server.
